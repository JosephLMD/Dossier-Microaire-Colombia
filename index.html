01 · Summary

The proposal in one page

We ask to be recognised as MicroAire's exclusive distributor in Colombia for the PAL line, and asks for four forms of support that convert a flat market into a growing one.

What we ask for. A certified service station in Colombia. Payment terms of 180 days. A structured surgeon training programme based in Bogotá. Support for marketing and congress activity, including half the cost of speakers.

What we commit to. USD 526,000 in the coming year with none of the above. That is the floor, and it matches what was purchased last year. With the four requests in place, USD 614,000, or roughly 28 units.

What it is worth to you. Held across a five year agreement, the difference between those two paths is approximately USD 1.51 million in incremental revenue to you. That figure, not our enthusiasm, is the argument.

The one sentence that matters. Every surgeon relationship MicroAire has in Colombia today runs through one person, and that person is the owner of ACT. This proposal does not move the channel. It formalises where the channel already is.

02 · The channel

Why the channel is already ours

The usual risk in changing distributor is losing the surgeons. In Colombia that risk runs the other way.

MicroAire's presence in Colombia has been built by a single person: the owner of ACT. She is the salesperson, the clinical contact and the point of continuity for every surgeon using PAL in this country. She also owns and runs microairecolombia.com, the only Spanish-language home the brand has here.

What changes with this proposal is not who holds those relationships. It is the structure around them.

03 · Service

What we are giving first: service

Before anything we are asking for, this is the item that costs MicroAire the most today and is invisible from Charlottesville.

A PAL handpiece is a mechanical instrument and it wears. With no certified maintenance in Colombia, a unit needing repair leaves the country and returns in about six weeks. For that period the surgeon has an idle theatre, and at the end of it a decision to make about whether to buy the next one.

6 weeks
Repair turnaround today. The handpiece leaves Colombia.
3 to 5 days
With a certified station operating locally.

International shipments for routine repair.

How it gets done, and why it is not a two year promise

A new INVIMA authorisation for a repair station in Colombia takes roughly two years. We are not proposing to wait for one. The station will be subcontracted to Nova Tecnica, an establishment already authorised and already servicing equipment of this class. That turns a two year regulatory project into a commercial arrangement, and it means local repair can be running in months.

The part that is not about logistics. Colombian surgeons are declining to renew and upgrade because there is no maintenance in the country. That reluctance attaches to the MicroAire name, not to ours. A local station repairs the reputation and permanently removes international repair shipping from your cost of serving this market.

Liposuction alone is 12.8% of every aesthetic surgical procedure performed in the country. Widen it to procedures where fat is aspirated and reused, buttock augmentation at 27,360, facial fat grafting at 14,304, and just over a quarter of Colombian aesthetic surgery passes through a cannula. Colombia is also among the countries with the highest proportion of foreign patients, which concentrates that volume into a comparatively small number of high-throughput clinics.

The competitive position

PAL LipoSculptor is the only power-assisted liposuction system still in manufacture. Wells-Johnson, Byron and Medtronic have all left the category. MicroAire therefore does not compete against other PAL devices here. It competes across modalities, against ultrasound and laser platforms. That is a clinical conversation with surgeons rather than a price argument, and it is the conversation we are equipped to have.

A risk that argues for exclusivity. Clone units are openly marketed in this region as substitutes for the 650 handpiece. A single accountable distributor with traceable serial numbers and authorised service is the only practical answer, and it protects pricing and clinical reputation at the same time.

05 · This year

Why this year was flat, and what it tells us

We would rather explain this precisely than let it sit unexplained. It was not price, and it was not the exchange rate.

The Colombian peso appreciated sharply. The representative market rate moved from COP 4,040.85 in July 2025 to COP 3,361.62 in July 2026, a fall in the dollar of about 17%. In peso terms a USD 20,000 console went from roughly COP 80.8 million to COP 67.2 million. Your equipment became cheaper here, not more expensive.

What changed was the cost of money. Banco de la República raised its policy rate from 9.25% at the start of 2026 to 12.00% in June, a rise of 275 basis points in a single year, and held it there on 5 August 2026. Annual inflation stood at 5.8% in May.

So the binding constraint is financing, not price. The equipment became more affordable while borrowing to buy it became materially more expensive, and MicroAire does not offer credit. In a market like this one, whoever carries the financing takes the sale. That is why the request for 180-day terms is not a concession we are seeking; it is the single most effective growth mechanism available to us in the coming year.

06 · The requests

The four requests

Each is tied to a mechanism rather than to goodwill, and each has a stated effect on the number in section 07.

A certified service station in Colombia
Repairs in three to five days rather than six weeks. Removes the most frequently cited reason surgeons decline to renew, and removes international repair shipping from your costs.
Payment terms of 180 days
So that ACT can carry the financing in a market where the policy rate sits at 12%. Separately, where we are able to settle an invoice immediately, we would like to agree a prompt payment discount, which improves your cash position rather than burdening it.
A structured training programme
Based in Bogotá, with local and international faculty, plus sponsored sessions at universities with plastic surgery specialisations. One-to-one access converts better than congress footfall and it seeds the next generation of users.
Congress and speaker support
ACT funds the stand at the national plastic surgery congress and at SAPS and TDABS, as it has under a three year relationship. For this coming season ACT has no budget allocated to speakers, so we are asking MicroAire to fund that side first. A surgeon speaking about PAL to an international room is worth more to the brand than to us, which is why we would rather MicroAire lead it than have it not happen.

08 · The plan

How the units get sold

Five mechanisms. Each names who it targets and who pays for it.

The market we are addressing

The Sociedad Colombiana de Cirugia Plastica lists over 950 certified plastic surgeons. Of those, 350 already use MicroAire, which is our installed base. Of the remaining 600, around 20% work primarily in facial and reconstructive surgery and are not candidates for PAL. That leaves an addressable target of 480 surgeons.

We should be straightforward about sequencing: the university programme will not sell a console this year. It is there because the surgeons who buy in 2030 are in a lecture theatre in 2026, and because nobody else is talking to them about MicroAire.

Why the year one target is conservative rather than modest

It matters that none of these mechanisms exists today. The 24 units sold each year come from direct selling alone. There is no replacement programme, no university programme, no financing offer.

The replacement programme is the clearest example. Against an installed base of 350 on a three year upgrade cycle, its capacity is roughly 23 units a year. The year one target assumes only 4 of those 23 convert, which is 17% of what the programme could carry. That is deliberate: a programme in its first year does not run at capacity.

It also answers the obvious question about the years that follow. The growth from year two onward is not an assertion; it is the remaining 19 units a year of capacity that the first year does not touch.

What we have already built

None of this proposal describes a standing start. The work is under way and ACT is paying for it.

  • microairecolombia.com, the brand's Spanish-language home in this market, built and maintained by ACT: product pages, clinical evidence, video library and a service section.
  • Eight surgeons published on the site as PAL users, in their own words and under their own names.
  • A direct advisory line answered within one to two hours by a person, not a contact form.
  • A congress stand operated by ACT under a three-year relationship with the two largest international meetings held in Colombia.
  • Published clinical claims with the study behind them: 31% more volume per minute, 35% less surgical time, 49% less surgeon fatigue.

All of it was built to make it easier for a Colombian surgeon to choose MicroAire, and all of it was built before anyone asked us to. What the four requests change is the scale at which it runs, not whether it happens.

10 · Portfolio

One distributor for the whole portfolio

PAL is the business today. Endotine is what makes ACT an integral distributor rather than a single-line reseller.

PAL, selling now. Power-assisted lipoplasty and the cannula family. The INVIMA sanitary registration is held by ACT and is current. There is no registration to obtain, no transfer to negotiate, no launch delay and no dependency on a third party to keep the product legally importable. This is where the 28 units and the USD 614,000 come from.

Endotine, registration in progress. Absorbable fixation for facial rejuvenation. Its INVIMA process is already under way at ACT's own cost and risk, ahead of any agreement between us.

Why this matters to MicroAire and not only to us. The surgeon who buys a PAL console for the body is the same surgeon who lifts a face. One distributor covering both means one relationship, one training calendar, one service channel and one forecast, instead of two partners competing for the same appointment. Endotine is additive: nothing in this proposal makes PAL wait for it.
